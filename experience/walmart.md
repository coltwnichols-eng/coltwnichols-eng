# Walmart | Transportation strategic programs

As a Senior Manager in Transportation Strategic Programs, I worked on national initiatives that cut across real estate, supply chain, transportation, capital planning, and field execution. My role centered on making large, ambiguous programs executable.

## Programs and mechanisms

- Built and deployed a **national infrastructure project intake and governance process**, giving teams a more consistent way to define requests, decisions, owners, and progress.
- Coordinated programs involving **40–50+ stakeholders** across Real Estate, Supply Chain, and Transportation.
- Guided the enterprise rollout of **modular trailers** to address space constraints and align operational needs with infrastructure plans.
- Connected Transportation to **long-range remodel planning**, bringing operating requirements into capital and site-level decisions earlier.
- Authored funding documents, white papers, and flash updates used in capital investment discussions and leadership decisions.
- Standardized documentation and reporting for execution visibility; helped rebuild Transportation and Supply Chain planning collaboration.

## What I bring forward

This work sharpened my approach to intake, prioritization, decision records, dependency management, and executive communication. It complements the hands-on data and systems work described in my [Amazon](amazon.md) and [Flix](flix.md) summaries.
