# Amazon | North American routing and operations

My Amazon work covered two connected areas: North American last-mile routing programs and earlier delivery-station operations leadership. The scale forced clear metrics, strong partnerships with engineering and field operations, and disciplined rollout decisions.

## Routing programs and data

- Served as a North America route-planning subject matter expert across a network of **400+ delivery stations** in the United States, Canada, and Mexico.
- Worked on **ETLM data pipelines for North American routing** and the measures used to evaluate route-planning performance. This is the specific ETLM area I am documenting further; no internal pipeline code or configuration is published here.
- Led or contributed to a portfolio of **16 North America-facing projects** with wider network implications, aligning operations, product, analytics, and engineering around implementation and measurable outcomes.
- Provided engineering design guidance and owned route-planning KPI mechanisms, including planned-versus-actual time-window compliance.
- Improved planned time-window compliance in work associated with **$41M in savings**. A broader set of initiatives had a **projected $100M annual opportunity**; that figure is a forecast, not realized savings.

## Field operations and launches

- Launched two delivery stations in the Houston area.
- Led operations involving **13 delivery service partners and more than 1,000 drivers**.
- Managed **100+ full-time employees** across robotics storage and inbound dock operations, using process and data mechanisms to improve execution.
